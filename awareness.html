Why Awareness Matters

Being aware helps communities take action to preserve ecosystems and resources. Awareness leads to behavior change.

Reduce

Minimize unnecessary consumption and waste.

Reuse

Find new uses for items instead of discarding them.

Recycle

Proper segregation ensures materials are recycled efficiently.

Take Practical Steps

  1. Plant native species in your area.
  2. Segregate wet and dry waste at home.
  3. Save water — fix leaks and use mindful irrigation.
  4. Participate in local cleanups or awareness programs.
