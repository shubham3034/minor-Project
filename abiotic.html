What Are Abiotic Components?

Abiotic components are the non-living factors such as sunlight, temperature, water, soil, and minerals that influence how ecosystems function and determine which organisms can survive.

Climate

Temperature, humidity, and atmospheric conditions shape species distribution and environmental patterns.

Soil & Minerals

The mineral content and soil structure determine plant growth and microorganism activity.

Water

Water availability affects life processes, population density, and ecosystem productivity.

Sunlight

Sunlight drives photosynthesis and influences biological cycles.
