What Are Biotic Components?

Biotic components include all living organisms — plants, animals, and microorganisms. They depend on abiotic factors for survival and interact with each other to maintain ecological balance.

Producers

Green plants and algae that produce food via photosynthesis.

Consumers

Animals that depend on plants or other animals for food.

Decomposers

Fungi and bacteria that break down organic matter.

The Role of Biotic Factors

Biotic interactions — predation, competition, symbiosis — regulate population structures and energy flow within ecosystems. Together, they create a dynamic, self-sustaining natural system.
